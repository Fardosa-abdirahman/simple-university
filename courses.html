The Department of Data Technology Analytics offers courses that help students understand data, computers, and technology. The courses combine theoretical knowledge with practical activities.

Students can study areas such as data analysis, database management, programming, web technology, computer systems, and information management.

These courses help students develop skills that can be applied in different workplaces. Students can use their knowledge to work with data, develop technology solutions, and solve real-world problems.
