The Department of Data Technology Analytics is focused on teaching students how to use data and technology effectively. We provide knowledge about data management, analysis, computer systems, and modern technology.

Students are encouraged to develop practical skills through exercises, assignments, and projects. These activities help students understand how data is collected, organized, analyzed, and used.

Our goal is to prepare students for opportunities in the growing field of data and technology. We aim to develop students who can use data to solve problems and support organizations in making better decisions.
