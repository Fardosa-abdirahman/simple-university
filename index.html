Welcome to the Department of Data Technology Analytics. Our department focuses on the use of data and technology to solve problems and support better decision-making.

Data Technology Analytics helps organizations collect, manage, analyze, and understand information. Students learn how to work with data and use technology to find useful information from it

Our department provides students with knowledge and practical skills that can help them build careers in data analysis, information technology, databases, and other technology-related fields.
